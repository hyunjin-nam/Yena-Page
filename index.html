Privacy & Data

Yena · Last updated: 20 September 2026

Summary

  • Yena has no account and no login. There is no developer server that stores your data.
  • Your entries live on your device and in your own private iCloud. The developers cannot see them.
  • If you use the AI features, the content you analyse is sent to Google Gemini.
  • We collect basic usage events only, never your health values, notes or photos.

Who is responsible

Yena is developed by Jakob Larsson, Hyunjin Nam. Contact: jinanam0116@gmail.com

What Yena handles

Cycle information, weight and daily numbers, notes, and meal photos. This is health data, a special category under the GDPR. It is used only to provide the app's features to you.

Apple Health

Your Apple Health data stays in Apple Health on your device. The developers of Yena do not store, read, or have access to your Apple Health data.

iCloud

  • On your device: entries and meal photos (resized before saving).
  • In your iCloud: synced between your own devices through Apple CloudKit's private database. Sensitive fields are encrypted. The developers have no access to your iCloud account or the data in it.
  • In backups: if you use iCloud Backup, app data may be included, under Apple's terms.

Gemini

Yena uses Google's Gemini AI to analyse meal photos and provide nutrition estimates and daily summaries.

When you use these features, Yena sends the information needed to generate the result to Gemini. This may include your meal photo and relevant information such as steps, weight, cycle information, goals, and notes.

Yena does not store this information on its own servers. It is sent to Gemini only when needed. Google may use submitted content under its own terms (see Gemini API terms). If you don't use the AI features, nothing is sent to Google.

Analytics (Amplitude)

We use Amplitude to count usage and errors: events such as "app opened", "screen viewed", "feature used" and error codes. Events are linked to a random device identifier, not your name, email or Apple ID. Amplitude may also receive basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address. Events never include your notes, meals, photos, weight or cycle data.

Your data

  • No account, no name or email collected.
  • No selling or sharing of data for advertising.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Health data is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you enter it. You can withdraw it by deleting your data. Usage analytics rely on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app; they use a random device identifier and contain no health data.

Your rights and controls

  • Access and correction: view and edit entries in the app.
  • Deletion: delete entries or the app. To remove synced data, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Yena.
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud for Yena in the same settings.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you can also object, restrict processing, request portability, and complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Yena is not intended for children under 16 without parent or guardian consent.

Not medical advice

Yena is a personal tracking tool. Its summaries, including AI-generated ones, are not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
